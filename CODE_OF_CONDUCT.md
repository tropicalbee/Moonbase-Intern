# Code of Conduct

Moonbase Intern welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

The goal is a friendly place to learn, report problems, and improve the project together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Give constructive feedback about the work.
- Explain unfamiliar concepts without mocking people.
- Respect different perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly targeting, insulting, or intimidating a contributor.
- Spam or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Scope

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the project.

## Reporting a concern

Raise a concern through this repository's **Discussions** tab or open an
**Issue**. Describe the behavior respectfully and provide only information
that is appropriate to share publicly.

There is currently no private maintainer contact channel. For concerns
involving private information, threats, or sensitive evidence, use GitHub's
reporting tools instead of publishing those details.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, issue warnings, or restrict participation.

The response will depend on the severity, context, and whether the behavior is repeated.

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as contributors.
